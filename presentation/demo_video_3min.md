# ~3 minute demo video — exact steps

Use this as a shot list while screen-recording the **emulator** or **physical phone**
(QuickTime / macOS Screen Recording, OBS, Windows Game Bar, or Android Studio’s screen
capture). **Turn on Wi‑Fi** so portfolio photos load.

---

## Before you record (2–3 minutes setup)

1. **Build & run** the app on the emulator (`flutter run` or Android Studio Run).
2. Wait on **Portfolio** until photos finish loading (spinner disappears).
3. **Close** notifications / other windows; **Do Not Disturb** on.
4. Start screen recorder: **lock to portrait** if you want consistency.
5. Optional: bump emulator **brightness** to 100%.

---

## Minute 0:00–0:35 — Intro + Home

| Time | Do | Say (example) |
|------|-----|----------------|
| 0:00 | App already on **Home** (or tap Home). | “This is our Challenge 2 Flutter app — a handyman portfolio for Group 5.” |
| 0:10 | Slowly **scroll** Home: hero → stats → featured services. | “Home has a hero banner, quick actions for call, book, and portfolio, stats, and featured services.” |
| 0:25 | Tap **Call** once (snackbar). | “Call shows feedback with a snackbar.” |

---

## Minute 0:35–1:15 — Services + Portfolio

| Time | Do | Say |
|------|-----|-----|
| 0:35 | Tap **Services**; **scroll** the grid. | “Services lists six categories with icons — all Material icons, no custom asset pack.” |
| 0:55 | Tap **Portfolio**; **scroll** slowly through **2–3 project cards**. | “Portfolio shows completed jobs with photos, category tags, and short descriptions.” |
| 1:10 | Pause on one card with **Before/After** chips. | “Some projects show before-and-after labels for context.” |

---

## Minute 1:15–2:00 — Reviews + Contact

| Time | Do | Say |
|------|-----|-----|
| 1:15 | Tap **Reviews**; **scroll** through list; **scrollbar** may show on the right. | “Reviews show ratings and testimonials — scrollable list.” |
| 1:40 | Tap **Contact**; **scroll** to the form; tap **Send** with **empty** fields. | “Contact has business info and a form with validation.” |
| 1:55 | Optionally fill one field and send, or skip to save time. | “Required fields show errors until filled.” |

---

## Minute 2:00–2:45 — About + architecture (short)

| Time | Do | Say |
|------|-----|-----|
| 2:00 | Tap **info (i)** in app bar → **About**; **scroll** bio / certs. | “About is separate from the bottom nav — bio, certifications, service area.” |
| 2:20 | **Back** to return to main tabs. | “We use a repository pattern: data comes from mock classes now, ready to swap for Firebase Firestore later without rewriting screens.” |

---

## Minute 2:45–3:00 — Outro

| Time | Do | Say |
|------|-----|-----|
| 2:45 | Land on **Home** again. | “Flutter, Material 3, six screens, tests and analyze clean — thanks.” |
| 2:55 | **Stop** recording. | — |

---

## After recording

1. **Trim** dead air at start/end (QuickTime / Photos / DaVinci / CapCut).
2. Export **MP4** (1080p if possible), H.264, reasonable bitrate for Zoom upload.
3. If course asks for **voice**: record narration in the same pass or add voice-over in an editor.
4. **Rehearse once** with a timer — aim for **2:45–3:10** so you are not rushed live on Zoom.

---

## If portfolio images are grey / icon

- Emulator must have **network** (Wi‑Fi on, not airplane mode).
- First open **Portfolio** and wait **5–10 seconds** before recording that section.
